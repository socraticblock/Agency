## Product, market and locked story

**North Star sentence:** “Make sophisticated technology feel simple, useful and extraordinary.”

Genezisi is an independent digital solutions studio, not only a digital business-card provider, a generic AI agency, or a live demo of unapproved client integrations. The three disciplines are **individually commissionable**: Websites / digital experiences; Automation, workflows, integrations and system engineering; AI systems and assistants where appropriate. Visual distinction should come from refined editorial craft and a coherent demonstration of useful outcomes, not empty decorative spectacle.

**Selected B→C direction:**
1. Centered opening: **“Your website is only the beginning.”**
2. A large green site-derived B “THREE DISCIPLINES” interface arrives from **right on desktop**, **below on mobile**, and physically pushes the *entire* introduction out of view (left/up). No two-column end state. B settles centrally, completely opaque, readable including all three disciplines.
3. The camera approaches the actual B structure with a sensible zoom cap. B's backing sheets/columns extend into a forest-green/charcoal **architectural space**—solid perspective walls, frames, floor/ceiling, near/far parallax and coherent occlusion. One stable central reading axis.
4. The existing third reading plane emerges as a complete distant surface, advances, becomes readable: **“Beautiful on the surface. Purposeful underneath.”** Visitors may stop to read; don't force long motionless scroll bands.
5. The cream third card folds modestly/recedes while the solid C stage advances from the left as a full-height, shallow-edged architectural plane. It should never become an unrelated giant dark box, a `G.` foreground slab, an empty screen, overlapping faded text, or competing triangles.
6. **Final C**: **“Made to be seen. Built to work.”** The primary title starts appearing **while part of the green room remains visible**, but only once the opaque moving edge clears the *actual rendered glyphs*. The final C layout and readable text remain intact; secondary content can come later. Faint G. is merely a deferred background motif.
7. The eventual site transitions into **genuine** `SelectedWork`, Services, Studio and Contact. The current standalone route only mirrors project destinations for proof; it is not completed React/Next integration.
