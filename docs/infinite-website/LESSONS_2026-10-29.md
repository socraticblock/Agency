# Genezisi Infinite Website — lessons from the 29 October 2026 visual-review loop

**Status:** Dated learning record, not a new creative authority, not visual approval. Follow the [North Star v1.2](./NORTH_STAR_v1.2.md) and the [Acceptance Gates](./ACCEPTANCE_GATES_v1.2.md). Applies to the isolated B → C Gate 1b prototype and reusable motion work.

## What the founder actually selected

The selected direction is **not** a series of stacked slides. It is a seamless, native-scroll, user-directed film inside the structure of a real website.

- The intro is truly **centered**; the B interface moves in from the right on desktop and physically pushes the whole opening off left. On portrait mobile, B moves up and the intro moves out above it.
- B must be **large, fully opaque, entirely readable and independently commissionable** across Websites, Automation and AI systems before moving on.
- B's existing layers and columns become the **geometry** of the interior, with solid surfaces, true visual depth and a stable central reading axis.
- The third reading plane is first a complete distant object, then advances and becomes a calm reading stop. A partial headline before its panel is visibly established is a defect.
- Cards should be pleasant to **stop on**, not force substantial scrolling through a wholly unchanged frame. Slow and fast scrolling must feel like a single continuous journey.
- C remains the distinct final editorial statement **“Made to be seen. Built to work.”**, and must connect spatially to the third plane. It is not a second unrelated splash screen.
- The C title should become legible **while part of the evergreen architecture still shows**, but never be clipped by the advancing cover edge.
- The concluding page must ultimately lead to genuine Selected Work and normal website content. The present standalone selected-work mirror is *not* completed integration.

## Visual defects observed and corrective patterns

| Problem seen in founder recordings | Root or credible contributing cause | Working correction / generalizable principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intro overlapped incoming B or remained in left column | Independent text and panel transitions; early two-column target | Derive intro displacement from B's actual transformed bounds; settle B centrally as the **only** readable focus |
| B vanished before all labels could be read | Opacity and camera trajectories uncoordinated | Distinct full-B keyframe, solid materials, camera fit before depth takeover |
| Huge green surface persisted without interesting change | Long stationary scroll segments; delayed third plane | Remap *physical* scroll to story progress. Users can pause voluntarily; keep short held poses |
| Another surface arrived as tiny text shards | Text revealed too early relative to card geometry | Show a complete distant **solid plane** first; introduce readable text only at sufficient size |
| Old B header/footer remained behind third card | Only body columns retired; metadata overlooked | Audit **all** descendants of outgoing interface, including labels, decorative elements and bottom meta |
| Architecture resembled flat stacked rectangles | CSS `overflow:hidden` on a preserve-3D ancestor; weak depth and occlusion | Keep clipping at the outer scene layer, preserve internal Z, and validate perspective in real screenshots |
| Third plane looked like a floating modal | Surrounding geometry too faint and disconnected | Make border, structural frame, lighting and perspective originate from the established B palette/grid; fewer meaningful planes |
| Third card disappeared and C text looked ghosted | Simultaneous opacity crossfade of full stages | Keep the third surface opaque while folding/receding, introduce C on a **solid** surface |
| A giant dark `G.` rectangle appeared | C was cropped to a floating central window, with oversized background watermark visible too early | Use restrained full-height architectural coverage; delay ornamental watermark until C's copy is legible |
| Cream triangles protruded during reverse scrubbing | Several competing fold planes and too aggressive card rotation | Simplify motion: fewer wings, smaller angle, one structural causal movement |
| Empty dark C area held before message | C text was gated until entire cover finished | Show the main C title sooner **once its actual glyph bounds fit under opaque cover**; secondary material can follow later |
| Screenshots looked sound but live video differed | Partial reconstruction omitted full CSS/layout and deployment state | Treat synthetic proof as an engineering aid, not acceptance. Review the exact full served route and mobile hardware |

## Engineering patterns worth keeping

1. **Pure scroll sampling.** Visual state is a function of normalized scroll progress and viewport/layout. Reversing and seeking must reproduce the same state exactly, not depend on the last animation frame.
2. **Native scrolling, never compulsory waits.** Hold distance should be measured in viewport-scroll units, not video seconds. Trackpad recording time is not a fixed motion duration.
3. **Geometry-first camera constraints.** Fit B using the actual card dimensions, viewport size and margins. Enlarging text past the viewport or masking B prematurely is not a convincing zoom.
4. **Correct stacking and occlusion.** Verify `clip-path`, `backface-visibility`, `transform-style`, grouping/flattening properties, shadows and masks together. CSS transforms that pass syntax checks can still look physically wrong.
5. **One focal idea at a time.** Side architecture creates scale and atmosphere, but must not be required for comprehension. Strong still frames matter as much as transitions.
6. **Legibility gating from actual bounds.** The main C heading can resolve early only after the advancing opaque boundary passes the rendered glyphs, using responsive margins; mobile needs its own evidence.
7. **Conservative performance.** Avoid WebGL/Three.js as a default; authored CSS depth can suffice. Performance claims require actual device testing, not a guessed frame rate.
8. **Technical QA cannot sign off art direction.** Contact sheets, passing assertions and a partial DOM prove narrow conditions. Founder approval requires video/real browser output from the intended tunnel SHA.
9. **Differentiate decisions.** *Founder approved the direction/increment* ≠ *founder accepted the finished moving result* ≠ *approved deployment*. Record each separately.
10. **Do not lose source-of-truth discipline.** Maintain North Star v1.2 as controlling. Historical V2 “The Signal”, the earlier v1.1 plan and card-product rules remain reference history, not current homepage authority.

## Tests to retain/repeat

**Minimum Gate 1b frames:** first hero; incoming B at 25/50/75%; settled opaque B; approach; first visible green architecture; distant complete third plane; readable third plane; first fold; early C title with green still visible; completed C; Work seam.

**Interactions:** mouse wheel, rapid and slow trackpad, native touch, reverse, pause, jump to intermediate positions, responsive resize, browser history, keyboard focus/inert behavior, skip Work/Services/Contact, reduced motion, no JS/static path. For served route, inspect desktop `1440×900`, mobile `390×844`, narrow `320×568` and short height; add real iOS/Android when available.

**Evidence labels:** explicitly declare *partial reconstruction*, *complete source served locally*, *tunnel*, *mobile emulation*, *physical hardware* and *founder accepted*. Do not blend them.
