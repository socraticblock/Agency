## Audit conclusion

The planned **Infinite Website** is the future Genezisi studio homepage, **not an additional product or a detached microsite**. The current commercial identity offers **Websites and digital experiences; Automation / workflows / system engineering / integrations; AI systems and assistants** as individually commissionable disciplines.

Earlier Genezisi V2 “The Signal”, flagship four-act and doorway documents preserve useful engineering findings but contain older creative instructions and claims of being “active” or “authoritative”. Several always-applied Cursor editor rules also incorrectly applied **digital-card-only market, pricing and print details** to the whole site, specified **Next.js 15** despite `package.json` declaring **Next.js 16.2.1**, or required effects incompatible with the founder-selected restrained B → C direction.

The remedy is **one current authority plus historical preservation**. No 2026-10-29 document converts a draft feature into an approved/production implementation.

## Maintenance rule

When the founder revises a **locked** decision, update the North Star **and** visitor flow/acceptance gates where relevant, and annotate any affected older instructions. When only a copy line, geometry hypothesis or timing changes, record it in the active gate without pretending the entire vision has been re-approved. Always maintain an explicit distinction among **specified**, **implemented**, **tested**, **visually approved** and **deployed**.
