## Interpretation

The pre-patch score is intentionally harsh because a 1/2 means a strong senior engineer could infer the right behavior, but the skill did not make the decision path explicit enough for an autonomous coding agent. A 0/2 means the relevant safeguard was absent or the validator demonstrably failed. The post-patch 60/60 is regression/doctrine coverage on this fixed suite only; it is not evidence that an independent model will execute every rule correctly.
