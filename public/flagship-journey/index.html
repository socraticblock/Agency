Four acts, one illustrative system. Website design and development: a responsive editorial website invites a conversation. Automation and integrations: fixed rules validate a fictional enquiry, map CRM record DEMO-1042 and prepare an owner notification, not sent. AI agents: the request “next week, an afternoon would be ideal” becomes a consultation intent and time constraint. The permitted read-only calendar.read_availability tool returns Tuesday 14:00 and Thursday 15:00. Calendar creation and outbound messages are disabled. Owner approval is simulated; the approved draft is ready for manual delivery. No message is actually sent, no appointment is booked, and no external service is called. All three services are independently available.
